15-Minute Lentil Bowl

A balanced, budget-friendly dinner made with pantry staples — high in fiber and plant protein.

⏱ 15 minutes 🍽 Serves 2 🌱 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups canned lentils, drained & rinsed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 cups cooked rice or flatbread, to serve

Steps

1

Warm the aromatics

Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add garlic, cumin, and smoked paprika, and cook about 1 minute until fragrant.

2

Add lentils and tomatoes

Stir in lentils and cherry tomatoes. Cook about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until warmed through and tomatoes soften slightly.

3

Wilt the spinach

Add spinach and stir about 1–2 minutes, just until wilted.

4

Finish and serve

Remove from heat, stir in lemon juice and salt. Serve over rice or with warm flatbread.
