About

Why I do this work

I became a dietitian after watching people I love cycle through diet after diet — losing weight, regaining it, and losing trust in their own bodies along the way. I wanted to offer something different: guidance rooted in science, not shame.

My approach is simple. We build eating patterns that work with your schedule, your budget, and your cravings — not against them. No food is off-limits. No plan you can't actually follow.

Since starting my practice, I've worked with clients managing weight goals, digestive issues, and simply wanting a calmer relationship with food — combining clinical nutrition science with the flexibility real life demands.
